# Agenda: Part 1

5 November, online, in English. Four sessions with three breaks:

**50 min** · ☕ 10 min · **50 min** · ☕ 20 min · **50 min** · ☕ 10 min · **50 min**

Each session mixes short theory, exercises on your laptop and discussion.
Bring the laptop you prepared with this repository.
In some exercises, a few of you share your screen and show the others
what your agent did.

- **Basics.** What an agent harness does; boxing an agent in a sandbox;
  your own Kubernetes cluster on your laptop; secrets the agent cannot read.
- **Determinism.** Changes through Git with GitOps; rollback; specs and
  checks that block a bad change.
- **Steering and sharing.** What the agent sees; skills; sharing them with
  your team.
- **Debugging.** An incident from first report to proven cause and fix:
  the agent gathers evidence wherever it can look, from cluster state and
  recent changes to code and telemetry; you check what it found.
